FEM for Electromagnetics: Overview

This series walks through the finite element method (FEM) applied to electromagnetic field problems. It starts with a 1D scalar equation to build intuition for the weak form and Galerkin's method, then moves to 2D vector field problems like magnetostatics, where meshing, material regions, and boundary conditions all come into play.

Each part adds one new piece on top of the last: a new governing equation, a new meshing step, or a new way of handling boundary conditions. The code for every part is Python, mostly built around Gmsh for meshing and NumPy/SciPy for assembly and solving.
